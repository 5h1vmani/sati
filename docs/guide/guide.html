Your data stays on your phone

  • {{icon:circle_check}}Sati has no account and no sign-up.
  • {{icon:circle_check}}Sati has no ads and no trackers.
  • {{icon:circle_check}}Your log, your practices and your settings are saved only on your phone.

What "no internet" means

Every app must ask Android before it can use the internet. Sati never asks. So Android does not let Sati go online.

Sati cannot send your log anywhere, even by mistake. Not to me, and not to anyone else.

You can check this yourself. Find Sati under Apps in your phone's Settings. It says No data used.

{{fig:no_data}}

Sati's code is also public on GitHub, so anyone can check it.

Keep your own copy

Sati also stays out of Android's backup to Google Drive. So nobody else keeps a copy of your log.

If you lose your phone, your log is lost too. To keep a copy, use Export in the menu of Sati's Log tab.

Why Obtainium?

There are three ways to get an app. Only one works well for Sati.

The Play Store

Sati is not on the Play Store. It is a small personal project.

A file sent on WhatsApp

It installs once and never updates. You also cannot be sure that the file is the real Sati.

This guide

Obtainium

It gets the real Sati from its own page on GitHub. It updates Sati by itself. It is free and open source, with no account.

{{icon:circle_check}}Safe updates

Every Sati update carries my digital signature. Android refuses an update without it. So a fake update cannot replace your Sati.

{{icon:triangle_alert}}About the warnings

Android warns about every app from outside the Play Store. The warnings are normal, and they are not about Sati. Steps 2 and 6 show them.

Questions

Does Sati cost anything?
No. Sati is free, with no ads and nothing to buy.
What does Sati ask for?
It asks to show notifications, for the sadhana timer. It may also ask to run in the background, so bells ring on time.
Does Obtainium use the internet?
Yes. It needs the internet to download Sati and to check for new versions.
Can I remove Sati?
Yes, like any other app. Removing Sati also deletes your log, so export it first.
What if I change phones?
Export your log on the old phone. Install Sati on the new phone with this guide. Then use Import in the same menu.

Need help?

Report a problem on GitHub. It needs a free GitHub account.

Add a screenshot, your phone model and your Android version. Add Sati's version too, from the bottom of its App info screen.

If a bell did not ring, tap Test bell in Sati. Add a screenshot of what it shows.

If Sati helps your practice, you can star it on GitHub. A star helps other people find Sati.
