New: a practice log in a third tab, Log. Keep a list of your practices, counted in minutes, cycles, done, percent or a scale of 1 to 10. A done practice can be marked not done. Focus is a score from 1 to 10 that you can switch on or off for each practice. Add past days to type in old records. Export everything to one file and import it back, or import a spreadsheet of paper records.
