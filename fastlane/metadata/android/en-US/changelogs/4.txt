New: a practice log in a third tab, Log. Count a practice as a duration, cycles, done, percent or a scale of 1 to 10. Mark any practice not done, set it to repeat every 2 to 7 days, and move practices up or down. Focus is a score from 1 to 10 that you can switch on or off for each practice. Add past days to type in old records. Export everything to one file and import it back, or import a spreadsheet of paper records.
