Sati rings a bell now and then during your waking hours. Each bell is a moment to stop, breathe, and notice where you are. The name is the Pali word for mindfulness.

<b>How it works</b>
<ul>
<li>Random rhythm: bells come at unexpected times, so the mind does not learn to ignore them.</li>
<li>Fixed rhythm: bells come on the clock, for example every hour.</li>
<li>Choose the interval, from 15 minutes to 2 hours.</li>
<li>Set separate waking hours for weekdays and weekends.</li>
<li>Pause for one or two hours. The bell starts again by itself.</li>
<li>On silent, the phone vibrates instead. During Do Not Disturb or a phone call, Sati stays quiet.</li>
<li>Use the built-in bell or choose your own sound, and set its volume.</li>
<li>Turn the bell on or off from a Quick Settings tile.</li>
</ul>

<b>Sadhana timer</b>
<ul>
<li>Time a practice such as breathing exercises, chanting or yoga, in up to 10 stages.</li>
<li>The bell rings once at the start, twice when a stage changes and three times at the end, so you can tell them apart with your eyes closed.</li>
<li>Sadhana bells ring through Do Not Disturb and silent mode, at your alarm volume. If Do Not Disturb blocks alarms, they play at your media volume.</li>
<li>Optional settling time, a vibrate-only mode, and a lock-screen countdown.</li>
<li>The hourly bell stays quiet while a sadhana runs.</li>
</ul>

<b>Practice log</b>
<ul>
<li>Keep a list of your practices, counted as a duration, cycles, done, percent or a scale of 1 to 10. Durations and cycles can have a daily target.</li>
<li>A practice can repeat every 2 to 7 days. Press and hold a practice, then drag it, to set the order.</li>
<li>Today shows each practice and what is left. A done practice takes one tap, and a cross marks any practice not done. Other entries take two taps. A tap on a logged row edits it.</li>
<li>A focus score from 1 to 10 is optional. Turn it on or off for each practice.</li>
<li>Add entries for past days, so old paper records can be typed in.</li>
<li>Export the log and your sadhanas to one file, and import it back. A spreadsheet of old records can be imported too.</li>
</ul>

<b>Calm by design</b>
The bell needs no taps. The app has no streaks and no statistics. The only notification is the countdown of a running sadhana. It keeps working after a restart, and it uses almost no battery.

<b>Private</b>
Sati has no internet permission. It collects nothing and sends nothing.
